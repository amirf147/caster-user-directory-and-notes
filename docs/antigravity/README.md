## 🔬 Subsystem Philosophy & Grounding

* **Epistemic Detachment**: Scaffolding is ephemeral. We maintain zero emotional attachment to our custom rules, scripts, or documentation architectures. The moment native platform evolution or cleaner systems emerge, old scaffolding is discarded without hesitation.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Telemetry**: Synthetic unit tests cannot measure cognitive ergonomics or real-world friction. The human operator's lived experience over extended daily usage is the definitive arbiter of system quality.
* **Gated Epistemology**: Major architectural changes must follow the [4-Gate Epistemic Gating Protocol](../../.agents/workflows/adversarial-architecture-review.md) before execution, anchored by the Single Source of Truth in the [Repository Brain](../context/repository-brain.md).
